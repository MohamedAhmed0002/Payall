ABOUT US // THE PAYALL PHILOSOPHY

PAYALL

Built for people who want to see what they pay for.

Streaming, transport passes, gym memberships, apps. Subscriptions are everywhere, and it's easy to forget when they renew. Then a charge you didn't expect shows up on your statement.

PayAll puts all your subscriptions in one place and shows your monthly total in Swiss francs, down to the Rappen. Weekly, monthly and yearly plans are all added up for you. No bank login needed.
